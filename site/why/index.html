The argument, layer by layer

Why we built it

Agents are starting to act on organisations’ behalf, and the first questions one asks about a counterparty — who are you, what exists, who can, should I, how do I reach you, how are you represented, how do you operate, how does value move, what happened — have no machine-readable answer anywhere on the web. Each layer is the answer to one of them.

The founder’s case for each layer is being written. The pieces below are planned — named here, and linked only once each is published, because a link to something that does not exist yet is the one dishonest thing a map of honest statuses cannot carry.

  • IdentityWhy Identity: Who are you? planned
  • GraphWhy Graph: What exists? planned
  • DiscoveryWhy Discovery: Who can accomplish this intent? planned
  • TrustWhy Trust: Should I deal with you? planned
  • GatewayWhy Gateway: How do I connect to the old web? planned
  • RepresentationWhy Representation: How is the org shown to machines? planned
  • ExecutionWhy Execution: How does it operate? planned
  • CommerceWhy Commerce: How does value move? planned
  • AuditWhy Audit: What happened? planned

Until then, the depth lives in the map itself: the stack, the principles, and the repository’s architecture notes.
