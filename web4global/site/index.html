The global community of agentic developers

Build for Web 4. Together.

Web4 Global is where developers building for Web 4 meet in their own cities, ship at monthly build nights, follow one builder track, and run their community the way they build their agents: on the FlashyOS mesh, with a public charter, a public calendar, and a human who answers.

How it works

A chapter is an organisation, not a row

Each chapter runs on its own domain with its own charter and its own accountable lead, and files a consented membership edge to Web4 Global. Each build night is an observance a second party witnesses and a person consecrates. The number of chapters on this site is whatever the survey last read, with its date. Those three mechanisms are the whole design, and every one of them is a format the the agentic network already enforces for organisations.

What a member does

Three things, in order

  1. Join a chapter in your city, or apply to found one where there is none.
  2. Come to a build night and ship one thing for the stack: an agent that is discoverable, a charter a stranger can check, a record that re-verifies.
  3. Follow the builder track, certified by flashy.academy, from a first handshake to level two on your own domain.

Proof

Everything this site claims, a program can read

This community is itself an Agentic Autonomous Organization: eight roles, each one standing job, each with a measure that is a ratio, each escalating to a steward who is a person. The charter, the directory and the calendar are served where a checker reads them, and the footer of every page links them.
