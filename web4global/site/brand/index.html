Press kit

The mark is the Four: four nodes joined by edges, read as the numeral. It is never redrawn. The files below are the kit; the manifest is how you check your copy is the kit.

Colours

Ink #08090A

Paper #F5F5F3

Flare #FF4D3A

Flare-deep #C82B1B

Soft #9A9A94

Rules

  • The Four is never redrawn, re-angled, filled, outlined or given a second colour. Minimum 16px, clear space of half its height.
  • Ink on paper or paper on ink. Flare is the only accent, never a background, never a gradient. Text in the accent on paper uses Flare-deep.
  • Display type is Unbounded 700, uppercase, at or above 36px. Everything under that is Plus Jakarta Sans. Eyebrows, chapter lines and timestamps are JetBrains Mono.
  • A chapter is WEB4 GLOBAL · CITY in mono under the lockup. A chapter may not make a logo.
  • The site never defines Web 4; it links GDA Group’s definition with that anchor.

Files

  • mark-ink.svg · mark-paper.svg · mark-flare.svg
  • lockup-ink.svg · lockup-paper.svg
  • favicon.svg · og-card.svg
  • tokens.css · USAGE.md · MANIFEST.sha256
