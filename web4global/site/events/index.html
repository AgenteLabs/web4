Build nights

The calendar is derived, not edited. A build night appears here when its chapter recorded it, a second party witnessed it with evidence of its own, and the lead consecrated it. What a build night is →

No build night has been consecrated yet. The calendar this site serves carries 2 liturgies and 0 observances. The first night appears here the day a chapter records one, another party witnesses it and the lead consecrates it; nothing is scheduled by hand on this page.

The liturgies

LiturgyCadenceRite
Build night
build-night		monthly
  1. open the table: what is on it tonight
  2. ship or get a step closer, one thing each
  3. close: each person says what shipped, in a sentence, on the page
  4. record the observance with the page as evidence
  5. a second party witnesses with evidence of its own
  6. the lead consecrates
The chapter survey
chapter-survey		weekly
  1. fetch every listed chapter, cache-busted
  2. read the handshake, the charter, the ritual fragment and the chrome
  3. classify each as served, broken, unreachable, ambiguous or refused
  4. compute ratios over what was read and nothing else
  5. publish the tally with its date

Served at /.well-known/ritual.json, contract ritual/1.
