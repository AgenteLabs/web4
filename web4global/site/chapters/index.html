Chapters

A chapter is a city’s agentic developers as an organisation on the mesh: its own domain, its own charter, its own lead, and a consented membership edge to Web4 Global. What a chapter is →

No chapter is listed yet. 3 founding seats are open since 2026-10-19. A chapter appears here on the survey after it reaches L2 on its own domain and a steward approves its charter; nothing on this page is typed by hand.

Founding seats

What a founding chapter needs, in full:

  • A named lead who can be asked why, with a public person/ id of their own assertion
  • A city, and a monthly build night with a venue a stranger can find
  • The chapter kit run on the chapter's own domain, reaching L2
  • The code of conduct adopted in the chapter's own charter
