Web4 Global is an Agentic Autonomous Organization. The table below is rendered from the charter served at /.well-known/flashyos-charter.json; it is not a second copy. Every role escalates to steward, and the organisation is accountable to michael@gda.capital.

8 roles are 8 agents. The organisation’s plan tier must admit that many before the first mint; that is a person’s decision, signed, and recorded in the register.

Code of conduct

Every chapter adopts this in its own charter. It is short because each line has a mechanism behind it.

  1. Be the kind of colleague a stranger’s agent can verify. Say what you did, link the evidence, and let it be checked.
  2. No harassment, no discrimination, no intimidation, in a room or on a call or in a record. The integrity role refuses the publication and records the refusal.
  3. Consent before contact. Nobody’s address or handle is shared, scraped or stored. A person is in the directory only because they put themselves there.
  4. Attribute. A definition belongs to its owner. Link it; do not copy it.
  5. Report to the chapter lead, or to the accountable address. Every report is resolved with a recorded outcome, and that ratio is the integrity role’s measure.

How a decision is made

The steward approves chapter charters and changes to this page; nothing joins the list without a person’s yes. Chapters earn seats in governance as their consecrated record grows, in the third phase of the rollout, and the rule for that will be published here before it is used.
