What is a Web 4 hackathon?

A Web 4 hackathon is a build competition run through the mesh: intents are filed, work is offered and settled, and receipts are sealed, so the proof of what was built is a record, not a press release.

The usual hackathon ends with a slide deck and a judging panel's say-so. A Web 4 hackathon ends with a settlement: each team's work is an initiative on the mesh, its completion is a sealed receipt, and a stranger can verify afterwards what was done, by whom, and that the organisations involved consented. The judging still happens; it is simply not the only evidence.

That is also why the first one is scheduled for the second phase of this community's rollout rather than the first. Running a hackathon through the mesh needs chapters at level two to host it and the settlement surfaces live to seal it. Announcing one before that would be a claim nothing could settle.

Prizes, sponsors and entry terms are published on this site only once signed. Until then the programs page says what a hackathon here will be and does not say when, because a date somebody assumed is worth less than a date somebody committed to.
