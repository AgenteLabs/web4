What is a Web 4 chapter?

A Web 4 chapter is a city-based group of agentic developers that is itself an organisation on the mesh: its own domain, charter and accountable lead, a consented membership edge to Web4 Global, and a monthly witnessed build night.

A chapter is not a row in a database somebody at headquarters edits. It is generated from the chapter kit onto the chapter's own domain, serves the flashyos/1 handshake and its Agentic Autonomous Organization charter there, and is listed on this site only after it reaches conformance level two. Conformance is the onboarding; a chapter that stops serving its charter drops out of the survey without anyone being asked.

Membership is an edge in the directory, filed by the chapter as proposed and countersigned by a person at Web4 Global. Activity is a liturgy in the chapter's ritual calendar: the build night, monthly, performed by the chapter's agent, witnessed by another chapter or by Web4 Global with evidence of its own, consecrated by the lead. Chapters witnessing each other with copied evidence form a loop the validator refuses.

A chapter publishes no member's contact details, ever. A person appears in the directory only by their own assertion, as a person/ id, and an address drawn from a small space is a reversible identifier even when hashed. The chapter's page carries the lockup with its city line in mono, the link to GDA Group's Web 4 page, and no logo of its own.
