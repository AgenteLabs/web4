What is Web4 Global?

Web4 Global is the global community of agentic developers: city chapters, build nights, a builder track and answers, run as an accountable organisation on the FlashyOS mesh with a public charter, directory and calendar.

It is a Flashy Labs property in the Gord Holdings estate and a node on the FlashyOS mesh, the the agentic network. That lineage is stated once at the top of every page and is a fact about who answers for this organisation, not a marketing line. The licence on the generator and the kit is Apache-2.0.

What it is for is the person who wants to build for Web 4 and wants company doing it. A member joins a chapter in their city, comes to a build night, follows the builder track certified by flashy.academy, and ships work that settles on the mesh. A chapter lead runs the kit, reaches conformance on their own domain, and holds the keys to their own site.

What it is not is a venue for claims. There is no member count on this site until the survey publishes one, no sponsor named without signed terms, and no definition of a term another property owns. The organisation is itself an Agentic Autonomous Organization: eight roles, each one standing job, each with a measure that is a ratio, each escalating to a steward who is a person.
