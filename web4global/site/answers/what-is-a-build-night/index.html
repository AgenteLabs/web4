Answers · owned term

What is a build night?

A build night is a chapter’s monthly working session: developers ship something for Web 4 in one evening, the chapter’s agent records it with public evidence, a second party witnesses it, and the lead consecrates it.

The format is deliberately plain. One evening, one room or one call, one thing each person tries to ship or get a step closer to shipping. No talks longer than ten minutes. The chapter's lead opens by saying what is on the table, and the evening closes with each person saying what they shipped, in a sentence, on the chapter's page.

What makes it a ritual rather than a meetup is the record. The chapter's agent observes the night with a link to that page as evidence. Another chapter, or Web4 Global, witnesses it with evidence of its own, never a copy. The lead, a person, consecrates it. A night nobody witnessed moves nothing: it does not appear on the calendar here and does not count in the survey.

The calendar on this site is derived from the merged chapter fragments, so a chapter that stops meeting disappears from upcoming without anyone editing a list. Each consecrated night is rendered as a schema.org Event so that a search engine and a model find the next one in your city.
