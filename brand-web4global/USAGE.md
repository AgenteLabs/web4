# Web4 Global — brand usage rules (draft 2026-10-19)

**The mark is the Four.** Four nodes joined by three edges that read as the numeral 4. The four nodes are the four conditions of Web 4 as GDA Group defines them (somewhere to be, someone to be, something to earn, someone to do the work); the edges are what a community adds — the connections. It is never redrawn, re-angled, filled, outlined, or given a second colour. Use the SVGs in `assets/` as shipped. Minimum 16px tall. Clear space = half the mark's height on all sides. It is **not the Flashy bolt** and must never appear in a lockup with it.

**Ground.** Ink on Paper or Paper on Ink. Nothing else. Flare is never a page background and never a gradient.

**One accent: Flare `#FF4D3A`.** On ink, Flare is used as-is for the mark, the GLOBAL lozenge and at most one interactive state. On paper, text set in the accent uses Flare-deep `#C82B1B` (5.04:1 on `#F5F5F3`, measured); the mark may stay Flare-deep on paper. Flare is not in the estate's accent table (mint, blue, violet, ember, gold, cyan) nor any property accent in use, and is reserved to this property.

**The lockup.** `WEB` + the Four + a `GLOBAL` lozenge, the same grammar as the estate lockups (word, mark, suffix lozenge) so the brands sit together on a page; the different glyph, face and accent are what keep them apart. Never set "Web4 Global" in the display face without the mark.

**Type.** Unbounded 700 for display at or above 36px, uppercase, -3% tracking, 0.95 leading. Plus Jakarta Sans for everything below. JetBrains Mono for domains, chapter codes, timestamps, and eyebrow labels (0.16em tracking, uppercase). Fonts are self-hosted at build (no font CDN at runtime), as the estate requires.

**Motion.** One gesture: the four nodes appear, then the three edges draw in 240ms. No rotation, no bounce, no morph. Always behind `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Chapters.** A chapter is `WEB4 GLOBAL · <CITY>` in mono beneath the lockup, never a redrawn mark and never its own accent. A chapter may not create a logo.

**Words.** Web4 Global is a community *on* the agentic internet; it does not define Web 4. The definition is GDA Group's and is linked, with the anchor "Web 4", on any surface that says the term (the estate term map, decided 2026-10-04). "Agentic network" is FlashyOS's. This property says both and owns neither.

**Provenance.** The site carries the estate provenance bar. The line is a decision for the owner: node ("A Flashy Labs company on the FlashyOS mesh") or capital ("A Flashy Labs company") — see the strategy page.
